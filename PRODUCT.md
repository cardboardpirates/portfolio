## Product Principles

1. Recruiter-first scanability: lead with concrete signal (years of experience, hybrid skill set, employers) over personal-brand copy, matching Pedro's own stated discomfort with self-promotion.
2. Evidence over claims: don't add case studies, testimonials, or project descriptions that aren't real; the résumé and future real work are the only source of truth.
3. Hybrid credibility: the site itself should demonstrate the design+frontend hybrid it claims, with solid visual craft plus clean, accessible implementation.
4. Honest incompleteness: while the work section is empty, say so plainly rather than papering over it with placeholder content.
